Think twice

{% lucide "alert-triangle" size=16 cls="callout-icon" %}
Carousels hide content
Anything past the first slide is rarely seen. For a handful of items a card grid shows everything at once and is easier to scan. Reserve the carousel for genuinely sequential media — photos of one product, say.

If you do add autoplay, provide a pause control and stop under prefers-reduced-motion: content moving on its own is a serious accessibility problem for some users.
