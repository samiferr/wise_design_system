Use cases

  • Choosing exactly one option from a small, always-visible set (up to roughly seven choices).
  • A field where seeing every option at once helps the user decide, rather than hiding them behind a dropdown.
  • Payment method, shipping speed, or a status change - any small mutually-exclusive set.
  • Above roughly seven options, use a Select instead - a wall of radio buttons stops being scannable.
