Reaching a token directly

A few tokens have no utility of their own — the shadows, the z-index scale, the motion and control-sizing values. Use the custom property in an inline style or your own CSS.

Printing

Detail and list pages are routinely printed in the kind of application this system targets, so hidden-print is worth applying to nav chrome and action bars as you build, not afterwards.
