Use cases

  • Parts of a whole where the total itself is the number people actually want to see - use the punched-out hole to show it.
  • A quota/usage breakdown (used vs remaining) with the overall total or percentage centered in the hole.
  • A compact status or category summary on a dashboard tile, more visually distinct from a plain Pie Chart.
  • Same slice-count caveat as Pie Chart - keep it to three or four categories before switching to a Bar Chart.

The total sits in a plain positioned <div> over the canvas - doughnut_chart() is a pie_chart() with the slices' cutout set, nothing more, so there is no Chart.js plugin involved in drawing the centre label.

The CSS-only alternative

For a single percentage, .donut-chart needs no canvas or JavaScript at all — it is one element with a conic-gradient.
