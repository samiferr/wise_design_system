Initials are the default rather than a generic silhouette — they identify someone at a glance, and most users never upload a photo. {% endblock %} {% block doc_body %}

Use cases

  • Representing a user or account in a nav footer, a comment, an assigned-to field, or an activity feed.
  • A list of assignees/collaborators shown as a compact overlapping stack (avatar-group).
  • Any place a person needs a visual identity even when they haven't uploaded a photo - initials are the default fallback, not a generic silhouette.
  • A circular variant for a more personal, social-feed feel versus the default square/rounded shape.
