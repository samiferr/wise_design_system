The on-colors

A filled button in light mode is primary40 with a primary100 (white) label. Dark mode lightens the fill to primary80 - at which point white text would fail - and the label becomes primary20. That's what the M3 on-* roles are for: always paint a container's content with its own on- role (on-primary, on-secondary-container, on-error, ...), never a hardcoded text-white.

}{% endverbatim %}{% endfilter %}

Writing your own filled component?
Pair the container role with its on- role - bg-primary text-on-primary, bg-tertiary-container text-on-tertiary-container. Hardcoding text-white works in light mode and becomes unreadable in dark.

Surfaces

M3 expresses depth with tone: surface is the page, and five surface-container-* roles step up from it - lowest, low (cards, sheets), container (menus), high (dialogs), highest (filled fields, chips). In dark mode each step is slightly lighter, which is how a raised surface still reads as raised on near-black.
