Use cases

  • Showing parts of a whole with three or four categories - a status breakdown, a small category split.
  • A quick visual proportion check where exact values matter less than relative share.
  • Not for more than four or five slices - the wedges become too similar in size to compare accurately; use a Bar Chart instead once the category count grows.
  • Prefer a Doughnut Chart instead when you also want to display the total number in the center.

Where the legend comes from

charts.pie_chart() turns on Chart.js's built-in legend and colors each slice from the shared series palette, so there is no legend markup to hand-author or keep in sync with the slice colors - hover a slice or its legend entry and Chart.js highlights the other.

OptionDefault
plugins.legend.position"right" - pass plugins={"legend": {"position": "bottom"}} to move it.
plugins.legend.labels.colorvar(--color-gray-700), resolved client-side so it follows the theme.

Accessibility

{% lucide "alert-triangle" size=16 cls="callout-icon" %}
Colour alone should never be the only way to tell slices apart - keep the legend visible, and give the <canvas> a role="img" with a summarising aria-label, since a canvas has nothing for a screen reader to read otherwise.
