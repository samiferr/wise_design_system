Typography

Roboto, vendored as a variable font, on the M3 type scale: five roles (display, headline, title, body, label) in three sizes each. Every role is one Tailwind utility that sets size, line height, tracking and weight together (text-title-medium, text-body-small, ...). h1–h6 map onto headline and title roles automatically, and body text is body-large. Sentence case throughout: M3 never uppercases headings or labels.

Shape

The M3 corner scale: extra-small 4px (text fields, menus, snackbars), small 8px (chips, badges), medium 12px (cards), large 16px (side sheets, FAB), extra-large 28px (dialogs) and full (buttons, navigation items). Tailwind's rounded-* scale is mapped onto it, and data-radius moves the whole scale at once: soft halves it, sharp squares everything off. Try it:

Elevation

In M3, elevation is mostly tonal: a higher surface is a higher surface-container-* role, not a heavier shadow. Shadows are kept for things that float - menus (level 2), dialogs and snackbars (level 3), the FAB (level 3). The five levels are --md-sys-elevation-level1..5, and the shadow-elevation-1..5 utilities.

Card elevation

.card, .detail-panel and the other panel components follow data-shadow: flat (the default) renders them as M3 outlined cards; soft and elevated render them as M3 elevated cards, with no outline and a level 1 or level 2 shadow. .card-outlined, .card-elevated and .card-filled pin one M3 card type regardless of the axis.

State layers

Every interactive component - buttons, cards that are links, navigation and menu items, tabs, chips - paints an M3 state layer: an overlay of its own content color at 8% on hover, 10% on keyboard focus and 10% while pressed. It's one rule in tokens.css, driven by a currentColor gradient, so it tints correctly on filled, tonal and transparent components alike. Disabled controls use 38% on-surface content on a 12% container.

Background

M3 neutrals are tinted by the seed color by default ("tonal"), so surfaces feel part of the palette. data-bg re-generates just the neutral palettes from a warm or cool hue instead - every surface-container-* role and the gray ramp move together, while data-palette keeps its primary/secondary/tertiary colors.
