Keep it honest

{% lucide "alert-triangle" size=16 cls="callout-icon" %}
Each sparkline is scaled to its own range, so two of them side by side are not comparable — a line that climbs steeply may be moving by three units. Always show the actual number next to it, and pass explicit minimum/maximum if rows must be compared.
