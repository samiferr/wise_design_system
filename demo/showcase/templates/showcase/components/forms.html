Rendering a real Django form

In practice you don't write .form-field divs by hand — include _form_fields.html inside a .form-stack and pass it the form. It walks form.visible_fields, renders the label/required-asterisk/help-text/errorlist for each field, and switches to the checkbox layout automatically based on the widget's input_type. Non-field errors get a .form-alert banner above the fields. See it live on Categories → New.
