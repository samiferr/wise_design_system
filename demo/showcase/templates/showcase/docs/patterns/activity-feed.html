A record's history

Who created, changed or deleted what, and when, newest first: one card per change, with a Field / Before / After table for an edit. As the last tab of a tabbed record (Overview, children, Activity) it answers "what happened to this?" without leaving the record.

Feeding it

_activity_feed.html renders what it is handed and does not care where the log lives (django-simple-history, an audit table, your own model): build a list of dicts and pass it as entries. Put the feed in a tab like any other child page, with _pagination.html under it for a long log.
