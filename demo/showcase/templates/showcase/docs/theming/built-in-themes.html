data-theme="dark" re-points the palette steps (and the handful of tokens that read a different step in dark), so every bg-background-layer-1, text-content-subdued or bg-accent-background in the component layer and in your own templates is right in both themes with no dark: variants. The Tailwind gray ramp is repointed at Lumen's gray scale and inverts the same way, so older text-gray-600 markup still lands the right way up.

The on-colors

A filled accent button in light mode is blue-900 with a white label. In dark mode the fill steps one shade lighter (blue-800) so it still reads as a bright action on near-black, and white text still holds 4.5:1. The notice (orange) fill is the exception: its label turns black in dark. That's what the on-* tokens are for: always paint a fill's content with its own on- token (on-accent, on-neutral, on-notice), never a hardcoded text-white.

Writing your own filled component?
Pair the fill with its on- token - bg-accent-background text-on-accent, bg-neutral-background text-on-neutral. Hardcoding text-white works in light mode and becomes unreadable in dark.

Surfaces

Lumen expresses depth with layers: background-base is the page, background-layer-1 is a raised panel, background-layer-2 a card or field, background-elevated a popover or dialog, and fill-neutral-hover / fill-neutral-down the hover and press fills. In dark mode cards and popovers sit slightly lighter than the page, which is how a raised surface still reads as raised on near-black.
