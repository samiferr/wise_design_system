Practicalities

AttributeWhy
preload="metadata"Fetches duration and dimensions, not the whole file.
posterSomething to show before playback — otherwise a black rectangle.
<track kind="captions">Captions. Required for most accessibility obligations.
loading="lazy"On an iframe embed, so an off-screen player costs nothing.
muted + playsinlineNeeded together for autoplay to work at all on mobile.
{% lucide "alert-triangle" size=16 cls="callout-icon" %}
Never autoplay with sound. Browsers block it, and where they don't, users hate it. If motion is decorative, add muted playsinline loop and respect prefers-reduced-motion.
