Typography

Source Sans 3 (Source Code Pro for code), vendored as variable fonts, on Lumen's scale: five styles in a run of sizes from 11 to 73px. Every style is one Tailwind utility that sets size, line height and weight together (text-title-s, text-body-xs, ...). Headings are extra-bold (800), titles bold (700), details medium (500), body and code regular. h1–h6 map onto heading and title styles automatically, and body text is body-m (16px). Controls are set in title-s or detail-m (14px). Sentence case throughout: Lumen never uppercases headings or labels.

Shape

Lumen's corner radii: 4px (checkboxes, tags), 8px (text fields, cards, alerts, nav items), 10px (menus and popovers), 16px (dialogs and large containers) and fully round (buttons, badges, switches, progress tracks). Tailwind's rounded-* scale is mapped onto them, and data-radius moves the whole scale at once: soft halves it, sharp squares everything off. Try it:

Layers & shadow

Lumen raises a surface by layer and border, not by shadow. The page is one fixed gray canvas, background-base (gray-100 in light; it is not a setting), and every surface sits on it: raised panels are background-layer-1, cards and fields are background-layer-2, and floating surfaces are background-elevated. In light all three are white, so a card reads as a white sheet on the gray page; in dark they step up from near-black. Shadows are kept for things that float and come in three: shadow-emphasized (the resting state of a raised control), shadow-elevated (menus, popovers, tooltips) and shadow-dragged (dialogs, dragged items). They are --shadow-emphasized, --shadow-elevated and --shadow-dragged; the older shadow-elevation-1..5 names are kept as aliases onto them.

Card shadow

.card, .detail-panel and the other panel components follow data-shadow: flat (the default) renders them as a layer-2 surface with a subtle border and no shadow, the Lumen way; soft and elevated drop the border and raise them with shadow-emphasized or shadow-elevated. .card-outlined, .card-elevated and .card-filled pin one card type regardless of the axis.

Hover & press

Lumen steps a color instead of painting an overlay: hover moves a fill one shade, press moves it two. A filled accent button goes accent-background → accent-background-hover → accent-background-down; outlined, quiet and list-row controls fill with fill-neutral-hover, then fill-neutral-down. Disabled controls show content-disabled text on a fill-neutral-hover fill (no fill at all for outlined and quiet ones). Keyboard focus draws the 2px focus-ring on everything.

Background

The background is fixed: one gray canvas (background-base, gray-100 in light) with white surfaces on it, and no setting changes it. That is a design decision, not a gap: every text and control color is verified against exactly this canvas, in both themes and every palette, by npm run build:palettes. Because the canvas is gray, text and borders sit one step darker than stock Lumen: the accent and status -content colors use step 1000, border-default is gray-600 in light, and the neutral fills start at gray-200.

Density

Components read these rather than hardcoding a height, which is what lets data-density retune the whole UI in one place. Lumen's heights are 20, 24, 32 (the default), 40 and 48px (--component-height-50 … -300). Comfortable is the 32px default, bumped up one size on a touch pointer; compact steps down to 24px.
