Use cases

  • A birth date field where explicit day/month/year selects feel more familiar than a calendar popup to the intended audience.
  • Forms that must match a paper/legacy form's exact day/month/year layout.
  • Any DateField where Django's built-in SelectDateWidget already does the job with nothing custom to add.
  • For the common case of picking a date, prefer the single native Date Input instead - reserve Split Date for when three explicit fields are genuinely wanted.

When to prefer it

For a date far from today — a birth date, an expiry year — a native picker makes the user page back through decades of calendar. Three selects jump straight to the year. For a date near today, the native picker wins; use Date Input there.

CaseBetter choice
Date of birthSplit date — the year is the hard part.
Card expirySplit date (month + year only).
Delivery date, appointmentNative type="date".
Filtering a reportNative type="date", two of them.

The widget emits three bare <select> elements; wrap them in a flex row so they sit side by side rather than stacking to full width.

ArgumentUse
